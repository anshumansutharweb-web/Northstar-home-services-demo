Our Core Offerings

Carefully Curated Services for Everyday Home Peace of Mind

From scheduled preventative tune-ups to immediate fixes, we provide dependable craftsmanship tailored to keep your living space at its best.

Home Maintenance

Routine preventative inspections, mechanical checks, filter cycling, and safety audits to keep essential home systems running smoothly.

  • HVAC filter & airflow assessment
  • Plumbing valve & leak inspections
  • Smoke & CO detector test cycles
  • Caulking & weather seal refresh

Cleaning & Upkeep

Specialized surface care, exterior pressure washing, gutter clearing, and detailed seasonal cleaning for interior and exterior spaces.

  • Full gutter & downspout clearing
  • Walkway, patio & deck soft-washing
  • Window sill & track detailing
  • High-reach architectural fixture dusting

Repairs & Handyman Help

Dependable fixes for doors, millwork, cabinetry, drywall, fixtures, and everyday household wear and tear completed with meticulous care.

  • Door alignment & hinge calibration
  • Drywall patch, sand & blend repairs
  • Trim, baseboard & moulding repair
  • Cabinet hardware & drawer slide tuning

Seasonal Home Care

Comprehensive weatherization, winterization, spring startups, and storm readiness tailored to changing regional climate demands.

  • Exterior spigot shut-off & insulation

  • Spring exterior drainage inspection
  • Pre-storm readiness walkthroughs
Our Operating Philosophy

Built on Thoughtful Standards, Not Shortcuts

We believe home service should feel calm, predictable, and genuinely helpful. These four commitments guide every project we take on.

01

Clear communication

Prompt updates, transparent project scopes, and direct answers before, during, and after every visit. You will always know who is coming and what is being done.

02

Thoughtful workmanship

Respect for your home, meticulous attention to detail, and work built to last. We protect your living spaces and leave every work area cleaner than we found it.

03

Convenient scheduling

Flexible arrival windows designed around your household’s routine. Timely text and email notifications eliminate open-ended, wasted mornings waiting for service.

04

Straightforward estimates

Itemized scopes with clear labor breakdowns and direct material costs. No artificial markups, no hidden trip surcharges, and no surprise add-ons upon completion.

Our In-Home Courtesy Standard

Every service visit includes protective floor coverings, clean footwear transitions, organized tool containment, and a collaborative final walkthrough before concluding work.

Homeowner Scenarios & Standards

How We Help Homeowners Stay Ahead of Maintenance

Every home tells a different story. Here are three representative homeowner situations that demonstrate how structured care prevents small issues from becoming costly repairs.

Scenario A · First-Time Homeowner

“Need an objective baseline inspection of our new house.”

Moving into an older property often means inheriting deferred maintenance. Our comprehensive seasonal audit creates an itemized prioritized action plan so homeowners know exactly what to tackle first.

Northstar Solution:

Biannual preventative checklist + prioritized mechanical audit.

Scenario B · Busy Family Schedule

“A running list of small repairs that we never have time to tackle.”

Between work and family life, sticking doors, dripping under-sink shutoffs, and loose railings pile up. We schedule a consolidated half-day handyman visit to resolve the entire punch list in one visit.

Northstar Solution:

Consolidated multi-repair half-day package with upfront fixed scope.

Scenario C · Seasonal Transition

“Preparing the home before freezing winter weather hits.”

Unexpected cold snaps cause frozen hose bibs and heating strain. Our proactive autumn shutdown insulates outdoor spigots, seals foundation penetrations, and clears gutters before snow arrives.

Northstar Solution:

Autumn weatherization checklist and perimeter drainage clearance.
